Ishaare Image Trainer

Drop a folder of hand-sign photos → get a ready-to-import training.json

Folder Structure Required

Organise your photos into one sub-folder per label. The folder name becomes the gesture label. Any common image format works (JPG, PNG, WEBP).

📁 my_signs/
  📁 A/
    🖼 photo1.jpg
    🖼 photo2.jpg
    🖼 …
  📁 B/
    🖼 photo1.jpg
    🖼 …
  📁 SPACE/
    🖼 …

  5–20 photos per label is enough. More variety (different distances, angles, lighting) = better recognition.

To import into Ishaare:
1. Open the main app and enable Training Mode (graduation cap button).
2. Click Import Training and select the downloaded training.json.
3. Or replace public/training/default-training.json to auto-load on startup.
